Decisions to react to

  • Navy exam header like the real JAMB CBT, gold reserved for Submit, the current question and the TECHMED tip.
  • Light and dark mode, with "Follow phone" as the default. The moon in the exam header switches instantly for night study; dark mode turns the primary to gold.
  • The calculator matches JAMB's: basic, single line, + − × ÷, √, x², C (clear all) and CE (clear entry). No trig or logs, so students practise with what they will get.
  • Palette and calculator sit bottom-left in the thumb zone; Prev/Next bottom-right; Flag sits beside the question number so it is tied to that question.
  • Submit lives inside the palette sheet (and on S), so it is never tapped by accident next to Next. On the last question Next becomes Submit.
  • Instant feedback puts each "why not" line directly under its option, then the tutor card: concept, steps, TECHMED tip. Report sits on every explanation.
  • Subjects score /100 each and the mock /400, with the percentage and time beside them.
